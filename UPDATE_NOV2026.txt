EZYPOS - NOVEMBER 2026 UPDATE
=============================

What is in it
-------------
1. Pending GRN works as before March again: a sale only goes pending when the
   whole shop (all stores together) is out of the item.
2. Stock listing "All stores" no longer doubles the stock (it was multiplied by
   the number of GRNs of the item).
3. Stock Transfer after GRN: after saving a GRN you are asked "Send these goods
   to the branches now?". The Allocate screen shows Received / Allocated /
   Available per item; type the quantity for one branch and the last branch
   takes the rest. One transfer per branch is created; the branch accepts it
   on Stock Transfers as now. Also reachable from All GRNs (orange button).
   The old Stock Transfer screen works exactly as before.
4. Customer Payment (Payments > Customer Payment) - new screen: cash, cheque or
   any payment method from Masters, split over several, a discount, settles the
   opening balance and bills oldest first, prints a receipt, and shows in the
   Cash Flow report on the day the money came in. (Old screen: cus-payment-old)
5. Masters > Sales Representatives, a Sales Rep dropdown on the sale window,
   and the rep's name printed on the bill as "Bill By".
6. Production: the screen no longer hides under the menu bar; "New Production
   (same gate pass)" now shows the new production number.
7. GRN: the last purchase price fills in when an item is entered (you can
   change it); GRN view and edit show the selling price.
8. Supplier Returns (Transactions > Supplier Returns): find the GRN by its code
   or number, list of all returns, and Edit.
9. Reports: Item Stock Report, Item Sales Report, Item Purchase Report.
   (The previous Item Sales page is still there - link at the top right.)
10. Excel export on the reports works again.

How to update (about 10 minutes)
--------------------------------
0. Back up first: phpMyAdmin > your database > Export > Go, and
   File Manager > your site folder > Compress > download.
1. Upload every file in this zip into your site folder (the one holding
   index.php), keeping the folders, replacing the old files.
   Do NOT touch application/config/database.php.
2. Open migrate.php in File Manager > Edit, put your own password in place of
   CHANGE-ME on the MIGRATE_PASSWORD line, save.
3. Open https://YOUR-SITE/migrate.php in the browser, enter that password,
   press "Run step 15". Every line says "done" (or "already there - skipped"
   if you run it twice - that is fine).
4. Delete migrate.php from the server.
5. Press Ctrl+F5 once on the system so the browser loads the new scripts.

Until step 15 is run, the new screens say so instead of saving.
